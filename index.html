OUR EXPERTISE

Creative vision.
Technical thinking.

Decorative work on elevators and escalators must accommodate moving parts, access for maintenance and the equipment's operating clearances. A finish is considered together with its supporting structure, fixings and surrounding construction — not as an isolated surface.

Drawings, material proposals and interface details are coordinated with the project team and equipment supplier before work proceeds. Final design and safety approvals remain subject to the responsible project professionals and applicable requirements.

Tell us about your project

Design & technical development

+

From cabin layouts and 3D perspectives to CAD details, design development considers panel joints, supporting brackets and fixing methods. Door travel, moving-part clearances and maintenance access are coordinated with equipment information and the project team.

CAD detailing3D perspectivesDesign coordination

Material & finish selection

+

Stainless steel, glass, veneer and flooring are considered for their appearance, weight, durability and suitability for the intended location. Samples, finish direction, edges and transitions are agreed as part of the project material review.

Precision fabrication

+

Panels, architraves and architectural metalwork are developed around approved dimensions and details. Cutting, forming and joint arrangements are coordinated with the selected finish, supporting structure and installation sequence.

Installation & site coordination

+

Site dimensions, access and readiness are coordinated with the contractor and equipment supplier. Installation follows the agreed drawings and fixing details, with attention to interfaces, protection of finishes and the project handover requirements.
