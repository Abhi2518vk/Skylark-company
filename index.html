Engineering Discipline

Our Specialized Expertise

Why vertical transportation aesthetics requires high-precision engineering beyond standard interior design.

Specialized Motion & Safety Requirements

Designing and executing decorative finishes for passenger moving equipment demands specialized expertise because dynamic elements—such as moving elevator doors and escalator step clearances—must comply with stringent safety and technical codes across their operating lifecycle. Conventional interior design methods cannot be applied to vertical transportation systems.

Pre-Engineering & Technical Solutions

Skylark maintains comprehensive mastery of vertical transportation safety codes and contemporary aesthetic trends. Prior to fabrication and onsite assembly, we provide complete engineering blueprints:

  • Standardized fixation methodology adhering to VT quality and safety codes
  • Detailed joinery and attachment engineering around dynamic moving assemblies
  • Detailed CAD technical drawings & 3D perspective cabin interior renders
  • Optimized material selection based on elevator type and client aesthetic goals
