A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before we
begin.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Share the product or service you need, your site location, approximate quantities and preferred timeline. Drawings, dimensions and reference images help us understand the scope. You can send attachments in WhatsApp after opening your enquiry.

Can finishes be customised?+

Yes. We can discuss materials, colours, panel patterns, lighting and other details around your design intent. Availability and suitability are confirmed during design review; physical samples are recommended before final approval.

Do you supply complete lift systems?+

This collection focuses on elevator and escalator aesthetics, interiors, cladding and fabrication. For a complete lift system or a maintenance requirement, contact us to confirm the available scope before planning your purchase.

Are the images completed Skylark projects?+

No. Skylark is a growing company. Photography and film on this website illustrate design inspiration and product contexts, not completed Skylark projects or customer endorsements.
